# Jungle and sky tutorial recordings

Both chapters use the shared recorded_tutorial presenter: composed first-frame entry, the same 84% backdrop, original Shift/close artwork, squash feedback, automatic dismissal, level pause/restore and independent watched flags. Natural entry hooks are JunglePossession and CanopyPossession completion. Shortcut 5 forces jungle replay; shortcut 6 forces sky replay, without clearing progress.

Jungle: six seconds of actual controller/rope simulation. The squirrel starts on authored ground, jumps, holds Shift to catch Vine09, builds real right/up swing velocity, releases into the configured upward boost and holds Shift again to catch Vine08. Assertions cover grounded start, first grab, physical swing momentum, upward release and next-vine catch. Camera follow resumes from the current view rather than an old cached hell position. No forced target/vine assignment is used for the demonstrated catches.

Sky: 8.483 seconds of the real heat and hail controllers. Six-second automatic heat charging runs at its normal speed; one original hail block enters from above the view. Shift begins only at full charge as the block approaches, and the original shrinking/melting sequence consumes heat. Assertions check full charge, active flame/melting, gradual shrink, complete melt and survival. No balance settings or production movement/fire/rope logic are changed.

Each metadata file stores press beats and held intervals. The Shift artwork stays softly tinted while held, returning to white on release; existing chapters have no held intervals and retain their previous appearance.

The green frame is sampled from the supplied image. The sky cloud frame uses a technical hole mask generated by tests/build_sky_tutorial_mask.gd from the enclosed central white region; the cloud decorations stay white. The source JPG and character art remain unchanged. The mask is a shipped image asset, not generated during gameplay.

Validation: native ANGLE controller recordings and jungle_sky_tutorial checks (shortcuts, freeze, press/hold cue, stage persistence, replay, close and cloud mask), plus visual review of both original frames. Local feature only; no main merge or upload.
